Top 9 Tips

Getting the most from every chat

💬

Nine habits behind our strongest chats. None of these need extra time, they're about how we use the time we've already got.

Tips Chat Moments

The Nine Tips

1 Reply fast, every time
Speed & availability

The first response matters most. Try to jump in as soon as a chat lands, before the customer's attention moves elsewhere.

2 Don't let it go quiet
Speed & availability

A short check-in while you're waiting on the customer keeps things moving. A chat that goes cold rarely restarts on its own.

3 Open like a person, not a script
Opening & tone

A warm hello and a genuine question set the tone. Customers can tell the difference between a conversation and a form.

4 Read before you reply
Understanding & accuracy

When Tommy hands a chat over, check the summary first. Confirming what's already been shared feels far better than asking again.

5 Get the details right
Understanding & accuracy

A confident, accurate answer on product and destination builds trust fast. If you're not sure, it's always fine to confirm and come back.

6 Spot the opening
Proactivity & sales

Every enquiry is a chance to suggest something extra, an upgrade, an add-on, a better fit for what they've described.

7 Notice what else they need
Proactivity & sales

Insurance, transfers, a longer stay, a different room type. Our best chats offer these without being asked.

8 Solve it, don't just answer it
Resolution & close

A good response deals with the whole enquiry, not just the question asked. Think about what the customer needs to move forward.

9 Never end without a next step
Resolution & close

Every chat should close with something concrete: a quote on its way, a date to follow up, a booking link. Leaving it open loses momentum.

A Few Chat Moments

A few situations worth a look, based on real chats but reworked so they're general rather than specific. The agent in each is called Anonymous Agent, this is about the pattern, not the person.

Situation one

When the details are already there

Customer "Zanzibar or Seychelles, 2 adults, half board or all-inclusive, beachfront, 6 nights, budget around R18,000 to R28,000 per person. Yes please, connect me with someone."
Anonymous Agent "Hi there! Is there a particular resort or package you'd like me to look into for you?"
Worth trying"Thanks so much, I can see you're deciding between Zanzibar and Seychelles for 2 adults over 6 nights around that budget, let me pull together a couple of beachfront options in each so you can compare."
Situation two

When it's more than one leg

Customer "I need flights for 2 adults: Cape Town to Mumbai on 3 December, Mumbai to Doha on 15 December, and Doha back to Cape Town on 22 December."
Anonymous Agent "Do you have a preferred airline for your trip to Doha?"
Worth tryingIt's three separate legs, not one return trip, so it helps to read it as three bookings to check and connect properly, rather than one destination with a single answer.
Situation three

When the news is hard, still use the brief

Handoff brief Anniversary trip, departing Cape Town around 9 November, budget R80,000 for 2, wants Italy including the Amalfi Coast, must include a sunset dinner by the coast.
Anonymous Agent "Good day, thank you for the message. Do you have your visas already?"
Customer "No, not yet."
Anonymous Agent "You don't have enough time to apply for the Schengen visa if you want to travel on the 9th. Can you change your dates?"
Worth tryingThe visa flag is right and worth raising early, it's the delivery that needs the brief. "Thanks so much for all this, an Amalfi Coast anniversary trip sounds beautiful. One thing to flag early: a Schengen visa needs at least 15 working days, so the 9th won't quite work. We could look at pushing your dates by two or three weeks, or I can suggest a stunning alternative that doesn't need a Schengen visa if you'd rather keep the date." Same hard news, but it uses what's already known and leaves the customer with a way forward.
Situation four

When the handoff already has the answer

Handoff brief Family trip to Mauritius, 2 adults and 2 kids aged 9 and 13, 7 nights, 14 to 21 January 2027, half board or all-inclusive preferred, budget around R22,000 per person. Tommy has already shared a shortlist of beachfront family resorts.
Anonymous Agent "Hi there! Is there a particular resort or package you'd like me to look into for you?"
Worth trying"Thanks so much for all that, I can see you're looking at Mauritius for 2 adults and 2 kids over 7 nights in January, around that budget. Let me check current half board and all-inclusive availability against the shortlist already sent and confirm what's open for your dates."
A small habit

WhatsApp: fewer, fuller messages

Where it makes sense, try to give what you can in one complete message rather than a few short ones sent one after another.

Nothing about how you chat needs to change, it's just a good habit to have front of mind as WhatsApp's pricing shifts later this year. Nothing to worry about, just worth keeping in mind.
